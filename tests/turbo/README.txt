HPC Whisper Turbo Test 2.0
by Kyriakos Christodoulou

Test 1 showed that both directions were held back by overload: the
handheld's speaker distorts at full volume, and its microphone clipped.
This test plays the same kind of sound at several loudness levels, and
with longer pauses between symbols (room echoes), to find the best
setting for each direction. Every sound carries known data, so the
recording shows exactly how much arrived intact.

WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER
  turbotest.exe   the test program for the handheld (version 2.0)
  turbotest.wav   the test sound (the program plays it; keep it next to it)
  phone-test.wav  a sound for the phone to play (microphone test)
  README.txt      this file

SETUP
  Replace the old turbotest.exe and turbotest.wav on the handheld with
  these two, and copy phone-test.wav onto the phone (replace the old one).
  Start turbotest.exe. The title should say Turbo Test 2.0.

TEST 1: PLAY TEST (18 seconds, handheld to phone)
  1. On the phone, open HPC Whisper. Under Diagnostics, tap
     "Record 30 seconds".
  2. Within 5 seconds, press P on the handheld.
  3. Hold the phone about 30 cm from the handheld, as when you type.
  4. Most of this test is meant to be heard: two sweeps, then a hiss at
     four loudness levels and two more hisses. Only the last two parts
     ("Silent") should be inaudible. Note if you can hear those.
  5. Send the recording from Downloads (HPC-Whisper-recording-(date).wav)
     as a file, not through WhatsApp.

TEST 2: MICROPHONE TEST (30 seconds, phone to handheld)
  1. Set the phone's media volume to about half.
  2. Have phone-test.wav open and ready to play.
  3. Press M on the handheld, then start phone-test.wav straight away,
     the phone's speaker facing the handheld, about 30 cm away.
  4. You will hear a beep, a sweep, and a hiss at four loudness levels,
     then quiet (the last part is silent to most people).
     "Loudest so far" on the handheld should rise when the phone starts.
     It is fine if it reaches 100 %: the loud parts are meant to find the
     limit, and the quieter ones will still be clean.
  5. Copy mictest.wav and turbotest.txt from the handheld's folder and
     send them.

Space stops a test. Q closes the program.

WHAT TO SEND BACK
  HPC-Whisper-recording-(date).wav (from the phone), mictest.wav and
  turbotest.txt (from the handheld), and whether you heard the "Silent"
  parts.
