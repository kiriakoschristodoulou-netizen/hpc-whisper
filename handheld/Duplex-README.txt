Duplex Test 1.0
by Kyriakos Christodoulou

Finds out whether the sound chip of this handheld can play and record at the
same time, in which order the speaker and the microphone can be opened, and
how long switching from one to the other takes.

Copy this folder to the handheld, run DuplexTest.exe, press Enter (or tap Run).
It takes about 10 seconds and plays a one second beep (keep the volume low).
Save (S) writes duplex-report.txt next to the program. Quit: Q.

Send the lines on the screen (or the report file) back.
