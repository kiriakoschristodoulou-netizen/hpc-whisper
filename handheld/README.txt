HPC Whisper 4.0
by Kyriakos Christodoulou

Type on this handheld and the text appears in any app on your phone,
carried by sound too high to hear. Made on, and for, the HP Jornada 720.

PHONE
  Android: install HPC-Whisper.apk from
  https://github.com/kiriakoschristodoulou-netizen/hpc-whisper/releases
  Open it, follow the three steps, then switch to the HPC Whisper keyboard
  in any app. iPhone: use the receiver page
  https://kiriakoschristodoulou-netizen.github.io/hpc-whisper/

HANDHELD
  Needs an ARM processor and Windows CE 3.0 or later. Copy this folder over
  and run HPCWhisper.exe. Put the phone near the handheld's speaker.

KEYS
  Everything you type goes to the phone. Arrows, Home, End: move (Shift
  selects, Ctrl jumps a word). Bksp, Del (Ctrl: a word). Ctrl+A C X V Z Y.
  Tab, Esc.
  Alt+S Send, Alt+B Back, Alt+H phone Home, Alt+W Recent apps (need the
  helper). Alt+R send again, Alt+N new page, Alt+K keys, Alt+O settings,
  Alt+Q quit.

Each text is sent several times, on two bands, because the phone cannot
answer. The title bar says how many times it has gone out.
