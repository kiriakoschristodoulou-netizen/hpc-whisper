Mic Scan

A test sound for the handheld's microphone. HPC Whisper, by Kyriakos Christodoulou.

  1. On the handheld, open Mic Scan and press Enter to start recording.
  2. Put this phone's speaker 2 to 5 cm from the handheld's microphone. Phone volume at maximum, silent mode off.
  3. Tap Start below. The test lasts a minute. Keep quiet and do not touch anything until it says Done.

Ready

The sound is a series of beeps and whistles, many too high to hear.
